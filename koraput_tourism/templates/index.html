The Koraput Coffee Trail

Cultivated organically at 3,000 feet elevation beneath towering silver oaks and jackfruit canopies. Today, over 3,000 indigenous tribal farm families cultivate 100% shade-grown Arabica coffee celebrated worldwide.

1

Fragrant Blooms (March)

Highland hills turn white with fragrant coffee blossoms smelling like sweet mountain jasmine.

2

Cherry Harvest (Nov - Jan)

Tribal women hand-pick glossy ruby-red Arabica cherries on cool mist-laden mornings.

3

Sun-Drying & Pulping

Naturally dried under Eastern Ghats sun on raised beds to seal sweet notes of toasted almond & cocoa.

4

Artisanal Pour-Over Cup

Savor fresh medium roasts at local coffee tasting cafes in town and take home GI-certified beans.

Where to visit: Coffee Board Research Center (Machkund Road) & Koraput Coffee Cafe (Town Center).

When to Experience Koraput

Every season paints Koraput in a completely different, unforgettable palette.

❄️ Peak Season

Winter (Oct - Feb)

Temperature: 8°C - 22°C • Crisp & Foggy

Best for watching the sunrise sea-of-clouds from Deomali summit. Coffee cherries turn bright red and the annual Parab cultural carnival takes place.

🧥 Packing tip: Warm fleece, thermal jackets, and trekking boots.
🌧️ Lush & Roaring

Monsoon (July - Oct)

Temperature: 18°C - 26°C • Vibrant Greens

Waterfalls like Duduma and Rani Duduma roar with peak water volume and throw dazzling rainbows. Putsil Valley meadows resemble lush Scottish highlands.

☔ Packing tip: Waterproof raincoats, umbrella, and non-slip sandals.
🌸 Blossom Season

Spring (March - May)

Temperature: 20°C - 32°C • Aromatic Hills

Arabica coffee plantations erupt into millions of fragrant white blossoms. Pleasant morning treks and evening boat cruises on Kolab lake.

🧢 Packing tip: Breathable cottons, sunglasses, and a sunhat.
